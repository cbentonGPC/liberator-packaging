Heritage · Discipline · Defense-First

Liberator Packaging is a defense and aerospace packaging specialist headquartered in Richardson, Texas. We design, manufacture, and test mission-critical protective packaging for prime contractors, primes' tier-one suppliers, and program offices across every branch of service.

Our Story

People running a 70 year old industrial packaging company saw a need for technical packaging support for growing aerospace & defense production. A heightened focus on cyber security requirements called for a company focused on security, engineering, design, and process-driven manufacturing. Liberator was created to respond to those needs.

The name is a deliberate choice. The B-24 Liberator was the most-produced American military aircraft of WWII — over 18,000 bombers made by manufacturing teams who had to learn how to hold precision tolerances at extraordinary scale, under wartime deadlines, with no margin for failure. That standard of work — repeatable precision in service of the mission — is the standard we hold ourselves to.

Liberator Packaging was established to do one thing: deliver protective packaging that meets defense and aerospace specifications, at scale.

How We Operate

Operating Principles · 04
01

Earn the Clearance

CMMC Level 2, ISO 9001, ITAR compliance, and an ISPM-15 stamp authority — we hold the credentials defense work requires and renew them on schedule. We earn your trust before the first pack is made.

02

Specifications Are Not Suggestions

We take quality and security very seriously. MIL-STD, ASTM, ISTA, and ISPM-15 standards are the minimum requirements we adhere to. If the drawing calls it out, the part gets it.

03

Engineer Before You Cut

Our in-house design team uses ArtiosCAD to model, prototype, and validate before a single sheet of corrugate or board foot of lumber gets touched. We take surprises out of the equation.

04

Produce to Perfection

Our discipline and quality standards dictate we produce the highest quality product we can in a scalable way. We invest in the right people, processes, and technology to make it happen.

A Note From Leadership

We build packaging for people who can't afford a failure.

The reason Liberator exists is simple: defense and aerospace work doesn't tolerate the same margin of error as commercial shipping, and the customers who run those programs deserve a packaging partner whose entire operation is built around that reality.

Every program we touch — whether it's a single prototype for a UAS developer or a recurring requirement for a prime — gets the same treatment: engineer it, test it, document it, ship it. We'd rather lose a bid than promise something we can't repeat at scale.
