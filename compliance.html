Certifications Held

Active Certifications · 04

Each certification listed below is current, registered to Liberator Packaging, and verifiable through the issuing body. Certificate copies and audit summaries are available on request as part of standard supplier qualification.

CMMC Level 2
Certified
Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification at Level 2 — the standard required for handling Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI) on DoD contracts. Self-assessed after going through the easyCMMC program with CloudFit.
ISO 9001:2015 Equivalent
In Process
Quality Management System certified to ISO 9001:2015. Covers design, production, testing, and delivery of protective packaging and crating across all operations. Audited annually by an accredited registrar.
ISTA Certified Lab
Certified
In-house International Safe Transit Association certified test laboratory. Drop, vibration, compression, and environmental conditioning equipment on site, with test protocols aligned to ISTA Series 1, 2, 3, and 6 procedures.
ISPM-15 Stamp Authority
Certified
Authorized to heat-treat and stamp wood packaging material under ISPM-15, the international phytosanitary standard required for export crates and pallets. Certified through the American Lumber Standard Committee (ALSC) program.
