Assembly& Kitting

Multi-material component integration built to drawing or engineered from scratch. From field-ready spare parts kits to complex multi-component packaging assemblies, every kit ships complete and compliant.

Whether you need a government-specified spare parts kit or a custom assembly integrating multiple packaging types, we manage the complexity so nothing ships incomplete or out of spec.
