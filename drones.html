DronesPackaging & Transport

From small commercial UAS to large military platforms, we engineer packaging that protects airframes, payloads, and battery systems — built to the requirements of the platform and the mission.

Drone packaging sits at the intersection of several hard problems: fragile composite airframes, sensitive electronics and payloads, high-value batteries classified as UN 3480 hazardous materials, and operational tempos that demand packaging you can reuse in the field. We design systems that solve all of it — custom foam inserts cut to the airframe geometry, corrugated or wood outer containers built to drop-test standards, and battery compartments that meet DOT and IATA requirements. Whether you're fielding a sUAS program or shipping large-format ISR platforms, we build the complete package around your platform.

Program Overview

Custom crate and foam systems for a small UAS platform deployed by a commercial customer. The scope included precision-cut foam inserts for the airframe and parts and a wood crate for added protection. All systems were designed for repeated field use with foam geometry toleranced for quick open-and-close access under operational conditions.

Field & Transit Cases

Shipping Outers

Corrugated or wood outer containers engineered to ASTM D4169 drop performance standards for single or multi-unit shipment. Sized to nest with the field case or foam-only interior configurations.

ASTM D4169 Corrugated Wood Crate Multi-Unit

Battery Packaging

Dedicated inserts and containers meeting UN 3480 and UN 3481 requirements for lithium battery transport. Designed to isolate cells, prevent thermal event propagation, and satisfy DOT and IATA shipment rules.

UN 3480 UN 3481 DOT Compliant IATA

Large-Format UAS Crating

Custom wood crates and skidded containers for MALE and HALE class platforms, long-endurance ISR drones, and large multi-rotor systems. Engineered for domestic transport or export under ISPM-15.
