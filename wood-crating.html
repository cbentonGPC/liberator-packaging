Wood Crating& Export Containers

Purpose-built wooden packaging executed to government and customer drawings. From HIMARS missile transport crates built to Army drawings to precision-machined packaging, we can do it all.

Built to spec.
Zero deviation.

We manufacture wooden crates and export containers directly from custom or government and customer-supplied drawings — materials, fasteners, dimensions, and markings executed to spec without substitution. Whether it's a munitions crate or precision CNC-machined ordnance packaging, our process is the same. Don't have a drawing? No problem, we can engineer a solution for you or create one based on pictures and/or specs.
