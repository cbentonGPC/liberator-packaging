MunitionsPackaging

We engineer and manufacture packaging for the munitions industry including 155mm and smaller caliber munitions. Liberator Packaging is one of the few defense-focused packaging companies that can produce this tactical packaging to meet today's growing demands.

Munitions packaging leaves no margin for error. Every container we produce is designed around the specific hazard class, handling requirements, and service life of the round it carries. Our team has direct experience across the full ammunition spectrum — small caliber through large-caliber artillery and guided missile systems — with a track record built on zero rejections.
