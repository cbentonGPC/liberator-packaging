CorrugatedPackaging

Mil-spec corrugated boxes, trays, sheets, and die-cut solutions designed for defense applications. We offer V3C, W5C, V11C, V13C, & V15C as well as conductive corrugated and Coroplast® options for conductive & moisture-resistant packaging.

Standard Boxes

RSC / HSC / FOL / FTC (Telescoping)

Standard corrugated box styles built to customer dimensions for defense and industrial applications. Board grade and wall construction — single, double, or triple-wall — are selected based on the load, stacking requirements, and environment of each application.

Corrugated Sheets

Single-Wall / Double-Wall / Triple-Wall

Flat corrugated sheets and pads for use across defense and manufacturing operations. Grade & fluting types are selected per customer request or based on compression load, stacking conditions, if the sheet is bearing weight or just separating layers.
